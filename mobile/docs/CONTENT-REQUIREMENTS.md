# Content addendum: requirements 64–69

This supplements `PRODUCT-BRIEF.md` with the additional requirements supplied in the conversation.

## 64. Short-form sports content

Create a dedicated sports short-video feed: record/upload, sports content, captions, sports/location/player tags, reactions, comments, sharing, saves, creator follows, personalized discovery, and local/trending discovery. Include highlights, funny pickup moments, skills, dunks, goals, tricks, training, game clips, challenges, and tournament moments.

Support vertical full-screen video, swipe navigation, autoplay, mute/unmute, engagement controls, creator access, and sport/category discovery. Connect content directly to profiles, sports, squads, related games, tournaments, locations, and invitations to play. Content must feed sports participation rather than become generic social media.

## 65. Player Skill Showcase

Keep showcase videos completely separate from general social posts. Add a dedicated profile section with multiple videos and optional sport, category, caption, and uploaded date. Support sports and position-specific abilities, training, athleticism, and game highlights. Make the section useful when evaluating players for competitive games, squads, and tournaments.

Showcases provide player-selected visual context, not an objective ranking. Never automatically assign a skill rating solely from uploaded videos.

## 66. Moderation and safety

Both systems need reporting, blocking, own-content deletion, inappropriate-content removal, admin moderation, and spam prevention. Respect controls over interactions, showcase visibility, comments, and tagging. User-generated media must not undermine platform usability or safety.

## 67. Retention loops

Social content: play → capture → post → engagement → sports identity → player discovery → games → play again.

Showcase: build evidence → demonstrate ability → credibility → player/squad discovery → better games → competition → reputation.

The loops complement each other while preserving distinct purposes.

## 68. Discovery

Use interests, location, social graph, squads, nearby activity, game/tournament participation, prior content activity, engagement, recency, and quality where appropriate. Do not optimize purely for watch time. The objective is a healthy sports community and real-world participation.

## 69. Digital sports identity

Games, XP, reliability, squads, achievements, Skill Showcase, and short-form content should collectively answer who a player is, what they play, how active and reliable they are, who they play with, what they are good at, what they have achieved, and what they create.

Implementation coverage and remaining work are documented in `FEATURE-STATUS.md`.
